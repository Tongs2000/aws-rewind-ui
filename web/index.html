How to drive this

    
    
      
  1. Press the big button five times. It runs, in order: scan,
        plan, diff, revert (dry run),
        revert --confirm. space does the same thing.
    2. 
      
  2. The strip under it is the real command being run. Open raw JSON to
        show the document every panel is drawn from.
    3. 
      
  3. The timeline is the agent's session, one dot per change. Drag it and the
        table below shows what each field held at that moment; drag it to the far left for
        the before-values. It never moves on its own. From step 3 the dots take on each
        field's state: red conflict, green restored, dashed grey unproven.
    4. 
      
  4. The cards are one per field. Click one: the panel on the right shows the
        event id that proves its before-value, the change chain, and the API calls a
        revert would make. Its dots light up on the timeline.
    5. 
      
  5. A dashed grey card means no resolver could prove the old value. Type one
        into it and the plan re-runs with --set; it comes back blue
        (ASSERTED), never green.
    6. 
      
  6. Simulate someone else's change (top right) makes a third party edit a
        field behind the plan's back, so step 3 finds a CONFLICT and step 4 refuses
        to overwrite it.
